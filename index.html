Welcome to Hoardr

A private catalogue for everything you collect — belt buckles, diecast, stamps, cards, ceramics, anything. Record each piece with photos, condition and value, then search, sort and export it however you like.

Three simple levels

Everything in Hoardr is organised like this:

🗂  Collections — the top level, one for each thing you collect. e.g. "Diecast Models".
📂  Groups — sections within a collection. e.g. "1:18 Porsches".
📦  Items — each individual piece, with its photos, value, condition & notes.
Add your pieces, your way

Tap ＋ to add a Collection, Group or Item. Each item holds up to 6 photos plus its condition, value, dimensions, tags and notes.

Pick a category — Diecast, Stamp, Belt Buckle and more — and Hoardr shows fields tailored to it (maker, year, scale, and so on). You can make your own categories too.

Find it, show it off
🔎  Search, sort & filter by category to find any piece in seconds.
🔳  Grid or list views — however you like to browse.
🔗  Share a whole collection or a single item with a private link — friends see a clean page, no login needed.
